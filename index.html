Philosophy

From models to systems.

Calling an LLM API is the easy part. I'm interested in what surrounds it — the retrieval that grounds a model in real information, the reasoning that decides what happens next, and the tools that let a system act instead of just answer. A complete AI system behaves more like an assembly line than a single function call.

Research / engineering project — not a medical diagnostic tool.

The project focuses on domain shift across hospitals and imaging equipment, evaluation methodology, robustness under distribution changes, and explainability of the model's predictions.

How I work
Curious. Confident. Experimental. Builder-first. Systems-oriented.

I learn by shipping — building a working prototype, then pulling it apart to understand why it works. Most of what I know about agentic systems came from getting a pipeline to fail in a new way and fixing it. I'd rather have a rough system running end to end than one polished piece that does a single thing well.

Learning
Through projects, not just courses — each one picked to expose a gap in what I understand.
Experimentation

Systems

Practicality
